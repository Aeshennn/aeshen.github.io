About Me

I'm an aspiring game developer with a foundation across the full stack of game creation — from low-level C++ systems to real-time simulation, 3D modelling, and rapid prototyping with game engines.

This year I deliberately pushed into unfamiliar territory: building a physics-based drone simulation, competing under pressure in a Blender competition, and shipping multiple playable builds. Every proj taught me more than any tutorial could.

I'm drawn to the intersection of technical systems and player experience — the place where a well-tuned physics parameter makes something suddenly feel alive.
